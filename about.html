About Us

Crowned by Yanga is an affordable everyday hair salon based in Johannesburg. We specialise in braiding, installs, and hair treatments for young women who want to feel confident and beautiful.

Our salon focuses on quality hairstyles, friendly customer service, and creating a comfortable environment for every client.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide stylish and affordable hair services while helping clients express themselves confidently through beauty and self-care.
